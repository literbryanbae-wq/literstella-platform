# Challenge

The LiterStella Challenge (야나완 - "야 나두 원서 읽을 수 있어") is a structured reading habit formation platform. It transitions users from the initial diagnosis to a consistent reading routine through social commitment and gamified tracking.

## Challenge Structure

### Goal Tiers
- **30 Days (Basic)**: The primary goal for the 13th cohort. Successful completion leads to a full refund/donation of the entry fee.
- **66 Days (Habit Extension)**: Focuses on solidifying the habit. Reward: 50% discount on the next challenge.
- **100 Days (Perfection)**: The ultimate goal for long-term mastery. Reward: Free entry to the next challenge.
- **90 Days (Legacy)**: Historical milestone from previous cohorts.

### User Journey (6 Steps)
1. **Payment**: Beta participation fee payment to ensure commitment.
2. **Application**: Strategic planning of the reading journey (Book selection, goal setting).
3. **Declaration**: Publicly declaring the challenge in the community cafe.
4. **Certification**: Daily logging of reading progress and verification URLs.
5. **Badges**: Earning milestone badges at 3, 7, 10, and 30 days.
6. **Hall of Fame**: Recording successful completions and long-term streaks.

## Dashboard Features

The challenge dashboard provides real-time feedback to maintain motivation:

### 1. Personal Tracker
- **Visual Grid**: A 30-day grid tracking "Done", "Rest", and "Badge" days.
- **Daily Actions**: One-click certification and streak tracking.

### 2. Live Status Board
Displays a global leaderboard including:
- **Participant Info**: Nickname and country.
- **Project Info**: Book title and current goal (30/66/100).
- **Progress**: Total count of certifications and current daily streak.
- **Status Badges**: Indicators like "Approaching 30 days" or "10-day hurdle".

### 3. Data Visualization
Uses **Recharts** to display:
- **Weekly Progress**: Certification trends over 4 weeks.
- **Reading Modes**: Breakdown of reading methods (Intensive, Independent, Transcription, Audio, Workbook).
- **Popular Books**: Top books currently being read by the community.

## Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame is split into two categories to celebrate different types of achievement:
- **Success Hall**: Focuses on specific book completions (e.g., Harry Potter series).
- **Challenge Hall**: Focuses on long-term consistency and repeat participation (e.g., participating in 11 consecutive cohorts).

## Technical Implementation
- **Frontend**: React (Vite-based).
- **Icons**: Lucide-React.
- **Charts**: Recharts.
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS with a "Dark Library" aesthetic.
