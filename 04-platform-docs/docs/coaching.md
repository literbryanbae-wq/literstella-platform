# Coaching Partner

The Coaching Partner (Master Coaching by Stella) provides professional, expert-led guidance for readers who want to transition from casual reading to literary mastery.

## Master Coach: Stella
- **Background**: An expert coach with extensive experience in English reading pedagogy.
- **Philosophy**: Focused on removing the "fear" of reading and establishing a personalized "reading identity".
- **Awards**: Recognized for excellence in curriculum design and student success rates.

## Service Journey (4-Week Intensive)

The coaching follows a structured 4-week path:
1. **Week 1: Introduction & Plan**: Finalizing the book selection and setting the foundational rhythm.
2. **Week 2: Habit Formation**: Overcoming the initial hurdle and establishing a daily routine.
3. **Week 3: Intensive Reading**: Deep diving into sentence structures and nuanced meanings.
4. **Week 4: Mastery & Future Path**: Evaluating progress and designing the post-coaching independent reading plan.

## Core Features

### 1. Personalized Curriculum Design
Every participant receives a tailored curriculum based on their Reading Diagnosis results. This includes:
- **Book Matching**: Selecting the right edition (Classic vs. Harry Potter vs. Custom).
- **Pacing**: Chapter-by-chapter daily goals.
- **Method Selection**: Choosing between intensive analysis, audio-led reading, or transcription.

### 2. Multi-Channel Feedback
- **KakaoTalk Support**: Real-time Q&A and daily motivation.
- **1:1 Voice Coaching**: Scheduled sessions for deep-dive discussions on difficult passages.
- **Weekly Evaluation Reports**: Data-driven feedback on reading consistency and comprehension.

### 3. Coaching Quizzes
Interactive quizzes designed to check comprehension without the stress of formal testing. These focus on:
- **Contextual Meaning**: Understanding vocabulary within the story.
- **Scene Analysis**: Interpreting character motivations and themes.

## Technical Implementation
- **Frontend**: Multi-page HTML/CSS site with a "Professional Corporate/Academic" aesthetic.
- **Interactions**: Standard HTML forms for applications and quizzes.
- **Lead Capture**: Integration with communication channels like KakaoTalk.
