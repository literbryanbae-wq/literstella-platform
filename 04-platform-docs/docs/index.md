# LiterStella Platform Documentation

Welcome to the central documentation for the LiterStella ecosystem. LiterStella is a comprehensive English reading platform that combines diagnostic tools, gamified challenges, and expert coaching to help readers master English literature.

## The Platform Ecosystem

The LiterStella journey consists of three interconnected projects:

### 1. [Reading Diagnosis](./diagnosis.md)
The starting point. A diagnostic tool that assesses reading level and psychological type (SAEX model) to provide personalized book recommendations and routines.

### 2. [Challenge (야나완)](./challenge.md)
The habit-building core. A 30/66/100-day challenge platform with real-time tracking, peer motivation, and gamified rewards (badges and Hall of Fame).

### 3. [Coaching Partner](./coaching.md)
The mastery path. Expert-led 1:1 coaching by Stella, providing professional curriculum design and intensive feedback for deep literary understanding.
